About Me

I'm David, a content creator and video editor who enjoys turning simple ideas into content people actually want to watch.

My creative interests span short-form video, lifestyle, fashion, automotive, product and social media content. I enjoy being involved in the entire creative process — from developing an idea and planning a shoot to filming, editing and delivering the final piece.

I'm particularly interested in the balance between creativity and communication: creating content that looks good, feels authentic and has a clear purpose.

I'm constantly experimenting with new visual styles, editing techniques and ways of telling stories while staying aware of how social media and digital audiences continue to evolve.
